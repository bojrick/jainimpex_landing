## **Design Considerations**

- **Typography**:
  - Headings: Use a bold, clean font like `Roboto`.
  - Body Text: Use a lighter font style for readability.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Colors: Blue (#3F5BE3) and Grey (#444).
  - Accent Colors: Golden (#FFD700) for Copper and Silver (#C0C0C0) for Aluminium.
- **Spacing**:
  - Ample white space between sections for better readability.
- **Animations**:
  - Subtle hover effects for buttons and links.
  - Smooth scrolling for navigation links.
- **Mobile Responsiveness**:
  - Stack sections vertically on smaller screens.
  - Use a hamburger menu for navigation.
