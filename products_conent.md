## **Product Categories Section**
### Purpose:
Allow users to browse product categories easily.

### Elements to Display:
- **Heading**: "Product Categories"
- **Grid Layout**:
  - **Category Cards**:
    - Image: Thumbnail of a representative product.
    - Title: Category Name (e.g., Round Bars, Copper Products).
    - CTA: "View Products" (links to the category page).
- **Categories**:
  - Round Bars
  - Aluminium Sheets
  - Copper Products
  - Flat Bars
  - Hot Dip Galvanizing Plant
  - And more...

---

## **Individual Product Category Pages**
### Purpose:
Showcase products in detail under each category.

#### **Content for Each Category Page**:
1. **Heading**: Category Name (e.g., "Round Bars")
2. **Product List**:
   - **Product Card**:
     - Image: High-quality product photo.
     - Title: Product Name (e.g., Copper Round Bar).
     - Price: Display price (e.g., Rs. 685/kg).
     - CTA: "View Details" (links to the product details page).
   - Grid layout with 3-4 products per row.
3. **CTA Section**:
   - "Request a Quote" button linking to the enquiry form.
